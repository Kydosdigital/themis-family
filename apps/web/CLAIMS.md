The attached public-website brief is the page/section acceptance baseline. Approved docs remain unchanged.

| Claim / behaviour                                                       | Source                                                             | Website treatment                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Visible agreements; Child/Teen experiences                              | docs/14_PARENT_EXPERIENCE.md, docs/15_CHILD_AND_TEEN_EXPERIENCE.md | Product previews, distinct age-appropriate copy                                  |

| Launch gates                                                            | docs/37_BUILD_SEQUENCE.md, docs/38_DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md           | Pre-launch only, no launch-date or enforcement guarantee                         |

Unresolved before public activation: legal entity/contact details; lawful bases and website-specific retention; live waitlist/support provider; approved safeguarding operation; verified App Store link; final prices/limits; permanent logo; approved published editorial content; real-device Apple results. None are silently invented.

Marketing does not expose enforcement-plan internals, staleness thresholds or circumvention details. Photography never serves as a customer testimonial.
