# Themis Family design-system rule

The approved visual direction is:

- white background and generous white space
- cobalt blue primary, approximately #2563EB
- mint secondary, approximately #34D399
- soft aqua support, approximately #7DD3FC
- peach accent, approximately #FFB79E
- light grey approximately #E5E7EB
- soft grey approximately #F6F7F9
- dark navy-toned primary text
- no purple
- Manrope typography direction
- modern, calm, premium, family-friendly, not childish
- no surveillance aesthetic
- no law-firm / Greek-column aesthetic
- no aggressive red-heavy parental-control aesthetic

Implement these as semantic design tokens rather than scattering raw colours through views.

Examples:
- background
- surface
- textPrimary
- textSecondary
- actionPrimary
- successPositive
- informational
- attention
- destructive

Status colours must never be the only way status is communicated.

The logo is NOT approved. Do not invent a final logo or bake a generated logo into the app without explicit founder approval.

If the Manrope font asset/package has not yet been selected and licensed/added, use a documented temporary system-font fallback rather than silently substituting a different brand font as final.
